# V2.9 — the interview gets composed, contextual, and a graduation

Seventeenth round, Adam's notes 2026-08-27 (the afternoon tail of the V2.8 message — the
morning half shipped same-day as V2.8). Two of these revise V2.8 work at Adam's word, both
recorded where they land: the Workbrain+ price and the tiles row.

## The items

- **VB-139 · Dividers, and the drawer starts closed.** A faint divider between the follow-ups
  section and the answer area; a second below the nav cluster. In the interview (after the
  tour) the lower panel DEFAULTS TO CLOSED; double-clicking the closed strip opens it to the
  auto height — the second divider's bottom edge — and once opened, the height follows the
  questions so the question-and-answer area is never obscured without the person choosing to
  scroll. Dragging stays free, exactly as today. This reverses the resting-peek default the
  drawer has carried since V1.2 — deliberately, for the interview only; Browse keeps its
  layout. The composition/geometry specs move with the law.

- **VB-140 · initiative_status becomes the fill graph.** "Where does this stand right now?"
  renders as a vertical graph of the initiative that FILLS by stage — Just getting started →
  Actively underway → Stalled or blocked → Wrapping up — fun, fast, on-theme (the
  VerticalPick/DividedLine grammar: a posture, not a mechanism). The four stored keys
  (`planning`/`in-progress`/`blocked`/`wrapping-up`) are untouched, so old answers survive.

- **VB-141 · Prompts learn their context.** Inside a repeatable, the AI Assist prompt names
  the RECORD it is about — "out of scope" for Initiative A is a different question than for
  B — and carries that record's own prior fields. Beyond that, a FULL AUDIT of every copy
  prompt in the product (assist/interview-me, reflect-tighten, the proof pair, the
  interpret rewrites): each one carries as much previously-collected context as honestly
  helps the response. The law that rides along: the person's content enters a prompt only
  on the surfaces where the exact text is one press from being read in full before it goes
  anywhere (the expander/ReadOnlyBlock chrome — V2.5's confirmed shape).

- **VB-142 · The reference-example prompt asks for three.** One prompt, three deliberately
  different examples, clearly separated for the paste-back.

- **VB-143 · The pickers get personalities.** `risk_tolerance` ("choosing between…") becomes
  three drawn-icon radios. The `how-i-communicate` six (voice_directness, voice_formality,
  voice_qualification, shape_structure, shape_ask_placement, shape_length) each get a fun,
  familiar presentation — no more plain oval chips — and the ones whose content is really
  two writing samples (the writing-example/preference questions) choose by SIDE-BY-SIDE
  EXAMPLES where the content allows. Same stored keys everywhere; presentation only.

- **VB-144 · The graduation.** Finishing the Context interview lands on a completion step:
  the file is ready to download NOW, and the Skills builder is UNLOCKED — the ceremony and
  the immediate hook, then Home. On Home afterwards: the Move-file tile reads **Download
  file**, and Download + Prove-it-works stand in colour, obviously active, where they sat
  dormant before. Derived from the file's real state, nothing stored (the welcome-screen
  law, at the other end of the journey).

- **VB-145 · Upload at the top of the UI.** An upload control in the chrome; the flow warns
  that overwriting erases the current file and offers download-current-first — or both in
  one press. (The import machinery is FileActions'; this is a front door with a seatbelt.)

- **VB-146 · Actions hides for the beta.** No builder behind it yet, so it goes — the Home
  row and its user-facing doors — until Context + Skills are walked end to end and the beta
  ships. Post-beta it returns. Scope question below.

- **VB-147 · The shelf rebalances (revises V2.8).** YOUR NEXT MOVE goes to THREE tiles —
  Download file · Prove it works · Redeem a skill — sized to the space. Skills Library
  leaves the cluster and becomes a full-span banner like Workbrain+: an invitation to
  explore Workbrain Certified Skills, same action-button grammar. Workbrain+ reprices to
  **"Starting at $1K / month"**, billed monthly — the $10k-at-once year lives on the
  billing page only (Adam: under promise, over deliver).

- **VB-148 · The paste spot stays warm.** After the copy, the reopened box keeps its glow
  AND takes the cursor — focused and active while the person runs the interview in their
  AI's tab, so returning is just pasting. (A response to their own press, not a steal.)

- **VB-149 · The list's left icons become the carets.** The right-side down-caret goes; the
  section icons on the left carry the disclosure — pointing down when open, right when
  closed, empty when not yet started. One glyph, three honest states, said aloud as ever.

- **role_for** — Adam likes the start; untouched.
