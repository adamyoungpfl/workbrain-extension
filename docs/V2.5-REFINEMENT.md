## THE FLAGS

**FLAG 1 · "A simple modal opens" vs the no-modal guardrail.** Adam's AI Assist design darkens
the app "like it's being paused" and runs a step-by-step mini-pane. GUARDRAILS bans modals but
sanctions **sheets, for short self-contained tasks — which this exactly is**, and it opens only
on the person's own press (never an interrupt, which is what the ban exists for). PROPOSED:
build it as a full-height sheet with a dimmed backdrop — visually Adam's pause, structurally the
sanctioned mechanism (focus contained the way Sheet already does it, Escape closes, narrator
paired). Needs Adam's nod since he wrote "modal".

**FLAG 2 · The assisted flag is observed state, stored.** Skipping the recheck for AI-assisted
answers needs a per-question `aiAssisted` mark. That is the product observing usage — but it is
FUNCTIONAL state that drives the flow, exactly the precedent `reflectedAt` set (also observed,
also stored, also flow-driving), not measurement of the person. Stored beside `reflectedAt`,
never counted, never displayed as a number.

**FLAG 3 · Short-answer thresholds must never shame.** The "(Recommended)" pulse is the
encouraging shape Adam specified — and GUARDRAILS' "no nudges framed as guilt" is the law it
must keep. Thresholds are a core table ([DRAFT] numbers, per question kind), evaluated live,
NEVER stored, and the assist wording on that path changes to the encouraging variant.

**FLAG 4 · The drag paradigm needs its keyboard.** The divided-line drag selector (VB-121) ships
with a full keyboard equivalent (Space/Enter moves an item across the line) and a
reduced-motion still — the a11y floor is not negotiable, and a drag without its keyboard is a
locked door.

### VB-119 · AI Assist — the name, the icon, the paused mini-interview *(FLAG 1)*

3. The response step: paste the mini-interview's output into the highlighted box IN THE PANE;
