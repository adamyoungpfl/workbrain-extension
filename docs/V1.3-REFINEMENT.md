Third round of Adam's notes, captured 2026-08-20 while V1.2 was still building. **Not started.**
Sequenced after V1.2 lands.

## VB-15 · Thinner follow-up bubbles
The deep-dive chips (VB-03) sit too loose — reduce the padding between the label text and the
bubble outline so they read as compact tags rather than buttons.

Watch the floor: `docs/GUARDRAILS.md` requires every control to clear 44×44. Tightening the
**visual** padding is fine; the **hit target** must stay ≥44×44. That's a real distinction —
shrink the box, keep the touch area (padding on a wrapper, or a pseudo-element that extends the
hit region). The a11y suite will catch it if this is done naively, which is the point.

**Accept**: visibly tighter chips; every one still ≥44×44 to the pointer and to axe.

## VB-16 · Shimmer, and expand-in-place
Two changes to the same control.

**Shimmer.** A coloured shimmer across the follow-up questions, to draw the eye to them — right
now they're easy to miss entirely.

Decide the trigger deliberately, because a shimmer running forever on every question is the same
problem flagged for the spinning status-bar mark: permanent peripheral motion next to text someone
is trying to read. Options, in order of my preference:
1. **A one-time attract** when the question first appears, then still. Draws the eye once, costs
   nothing after.
2. **On hover/focus only.** Zero motion at rest.
3. **Continuous.** What "shimmer effect" most literally means, and the one I'd argue against.
Build (1) unless told otherwise; leave the others one constant away. Reduced motion: no shimmer,
and the chips must remain equally discoverable — likely a static tint doing the same job.

**Expand in place.** Clicking a follow-up question **expands that bubble into its answer**, and
**the other follow-ups disappear** while it's open. Closing it brings them back. This replaces
today's behaviour (answer appears below, siblings stay put).

Real considerations:
- The other chips leaving changes the layout height mid-interaction. Animate it, don't jump.
- Focus must stay on the control that was clicked, which is now the expanded bubble. Do not let
  focus fall to `body` when its siblings unmount.
- Keep `aria-expanded` correct, and make sure the answer is still announced when it opens.

**Accept**: one chip expands to its answer, siblings animate out, closing restores them; focus never
lost; axe-clean; reduced motion still discoverable.

## VB-17 · The question area fills the top 60%
Today the question sits high and leaves a wide blank band before the drawer — visible in the V1.1
smoke screenshots, and the reason this note exists. The question, its controls **and its answer
area** together should occupy roughly the **top 60%** of the panel, so the screen reads as composed
rather than half-empty.

This is a composition rule, not a fixed pixel value, and it interacts with two things that just
shipped:
- **VB-11** docked the nav to the drawer's top edge.
- **VB-12** made the drawer height continuously draggable.
So "the top 60%" has to hold as the drawer moves, and degrade sensibly at the extremes. Express it
as a layout that distributes space, not as a magic number — and check it against the longest
question in the ported data (`peeves`, `guardrails_list` and the `voice_*` questions with inline
worked examples are the tall ones) as well as the shortest (`preferred_name`).

**Accept**: no large dead band under a short question; the tallest real question still fits without
clipping at the default drawer height; holds at min and max drawer height.

## VB-18 · Narrate and mic toggles
Two icon toggles in the top section, **above the progress bar, right-justified**.

**This was deliberately deferred, and is now being un-deferred.** `docs/RELEASE-1.md`'s exclusion
list says *"narrator/mic (ship the toggles disabled or omit them)."* That was a Release 1 scoping
call; V1.3 is past it. Worth stating plainly so nobody later reads the exclusion and assumes this
was an accident.

**Three things are already in place for it:**
- `Prefs.narrator` and `Prefs.mic` already exist in `src/schema/storage.types.ts`, and `wb:prefs`
  lives in **sync** storage — so these settle across devices, unlike answers.
- `docs/GUARDRAILS.md` names the home: *"Two toggles live in the header; everything else is a
  default we chose."* These are those two toggles. This is the sanctioned design, not a new surface.
- The sibling app has a working reference implementation using the Web Speech APIs
  (`SpeechSynthesis` + `SpeechRecognition`) in `../modelcitizen/src/components/WorkBrainContextInterview.tsx`,
  including the TypeScript shims for `SpeechRecognition`, which is still not in TS's default DOM lib.

**Two things that need deciding before this is built:**

1. **The mic almost certainly needs a permission, and R1-13 is the store submission.**
   `SpeechRecognition` / `getUserMedia` in an extension page triggers a capture permission. Every
   guardrail about permissions applies: nothing beyond `storage` and `sidePanel` at install, and
   anything else requested in context, never bundled. Sequencing matters — adding a microphone to
   the listing right as the store package is being assembled complicates the single-purpose
   statement and the "collects nothing" claim at the worst possible moment. **Strong
   recommendation: ship the narrator first, and gate the mic behind its own decision.** The two are
   separable and only one of them touches permissions.

2. **Which voice.** Native browser TTS is free, offline, and works on a plane — which the guardrails
   require of every feature. Adam has expressed wanting ElevenLabs instead for the sibling app's
   interview, which needs a server-side proxy and an API key — that would be this product's first
   server dependency and its first feature that fails without a network. That is a much larger
   decision than an icon in a header, and it should not be made implicitly by building it.

**Accept (narrator)**: a toggle in the header reads and writes `Prefs.narrator` in sync storage;
narration reads the current question and stops the moment the person advances, skips, or toggles it
off; nothing is narrated that the person did not choose to hear; works offline; ≥44×44 with a real
accessible name and a visible state, not colour alone.

**Accept (mic)**: deferred pending decision 1.
