## THE FLAGS (collisions, and how each was settled)

**FLAG 1 · "Pop up a thin action box" vs the no-modal guardrail.** Settled by Adam (decision 4):
VB-107 is an **anchored, non-modal popover** — floats beside the button, never traps focus,
Escape/outside-click dismisses, auto-dismisses on copy. Not a modal, not a coach-mark: it opens
only on the person's own press.

**FLAG 2 · Color-coded stale dates vs nothing-by-color-alone.** The freshness date past its
half-life gets color PLUS a second signal (a small dot + the word carried in the accessible name).
Non-negotiable; designed in, not bolted on.

**FLAG 3 · New services vs the verbatim-port boundary.** `proofSource.ts` is a port snapshot.
Grok and Perplexity join via the overrides seam (an additions list merged by the adapter), never
by editing the snapshot. Their attach tips are authored here, `[DRAFT]`, same voice.

**FLAG 4 · Icon-only themed chips vs a11y.** Personas are design-only (decision 3) — color +
iconography, no printed persona name. The chip's accessible name stays the service name; the
persona never carries meaning the label doesn't.

**FLAG 5 · The vertical picker vs no-auto-advance.** VB-108 stays chips + Next. Animation gets a
reduced-motion still that carries the same information.

### VB-109 · The name questions get a generator
preferred_name and professional_name lose examples-as-suggestions and gain a **random name

**BUILT 2026-08-26 (Track A).** `core/flow/nameGenerator.ts`: 12 famous
