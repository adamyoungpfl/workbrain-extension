# V2.6 — Home becomes the reset place, and the splash becomes pure wow

Fifteenth round, captured 2026-08-26. The source of truth for the look is Adam's template at
`docs/workbrain-panel.html` — **the design style, not a literal port**: Adam's own framing is
that the template "contains some elements that have been changed in the iterations", so current

## The intent, in one line each
- **Home** gets the template's unified card-world look — a calm surface on its own cooler
  canvas, deliberately DIFFERENTIATED from the question canvas's textured wall — "a good place
  to reset from throughout the app."
- **Splash** loses its buttons: any click (or key) lands on Home. The surface itself stays,
  reserved for "something special to wow on app load" — a later, deliberate pass.

## The design language being adopted (from the template)
Card-on-canvas: one white surface panel on a cool ground, 1px `--line` borders, 14–18px radii,
soft two-layer shadow, staggered rise-in entrance (reduced-motion: instant). A slim chrome bar
(mark + "Workbrain · Model Citizen"). The file lockup with the brand glyph and a live meta line.
The four-step utilization meter as the page's signature. Two-up file cards with per-file accent
chips, status dot + hairline progress, and a verb ("Review", "Continue"). Actions as a dashed
locked row. A 4-up utility tile row. A gradient services CTA. The trust foot.

## What current Home has that the template does not (all of it survives, restyled)
The welcome/empty state for a fresh install (the primary start CTA); the freshness/next-move
recommendation card; the import path ("I already have a file", which also absorbs the splash's
former load door); the multiples entry where it exists; "Talk to a person". The template's
happy-path layout is the finished-state; every current state maps into the same grammar
(cards at their real states, meter at its real number).

**FLAG 1 · "Gate 2 · Repeat" prints the internal word.** CLAUDE.md: the four steps are

**FLAG 2 · "AI-utilized" overclaims.** The % is derived from authored answers (real, honest);

**FLAG 3 · Prices in the extension.** The template's CTA prints "$1,000 / mo" and

**FLAG 5 · The dark theme.** The template ships a full dark palette + toggle. The app is

**FLAG 7 · The meta line's claims must be derivable.** "workbrain.zip · v3 · 84 KB" implies a
zip bundle and a version the product doesn't have. PROPOSED: real numbers only — "3 files ·

**FLAG 8 · The byline.** Template: "The AI operating system that travels with you." The locked

the splash/store. DECISION: which line sits under the lockup — template's, the tagline, or the

**FLAG 9 · "See what's stored".** A trust feature worth having: PROPOSED as a small sheet
listing the storage keys in plain words (what each holds, nothing else). Cheap, honest, very

**FLAG 10 · Home's ground vs the wall.** The differentiation Adam asked for: Home paints its
own opaque cool canvas (the template's `--canvas` + radial tint, tokenized); the breathing wall
remains the INTERVIEW's ground. The reset-place feeling is exactly this contrast. PROPOSED yes.

## The utilization formula (VB-125a, pinned)
Four segments, 25 points each, every input authored or derived-at-render:
- **Name** = Context interview completion (finished sections over all sections — the existing
  section-status logic, no new stamps).
- **Repeat** = Skills completion (each authored skill's filled required fields over its total;
  zero skills = 0).
- **Act** = the fraction of authored skills whose act-able answers are real (automation
  fields answered and not "not sure" — the Actions.md derivation's own inputs).
- **Share** = outside-work evidence that already exists for functional reasons: the proof
  loop's self-reported scores (authored) and minted recordIds from a pack save/share (stored
  as identity, read here as "sharing began"). Partial by design; the ceiling is real.
A person who finishes everything solo lands in the 50–75% band; the meter tells the truth
about why the rest exists. Exact math in `src/core/home/utilization.ts` with fixture tests.
